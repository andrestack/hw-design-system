# LinkedIn caption: Telegram voice-note bot

A local pool shop kept losing messages.

Customer requests read too late. Repair notes buried in one of the team's apps. Things said on a phone call and never written down.

So I built them a Telegram bot that only accepts voice notes.

The tech talks. The bot writes it up, sorts it into a task or an enquiry, and drops it on the admin's board. The admin keeps an eye on the board, actions things and moves them along.

The part I didn't expect: technical detail stopped getting lost in translation. If you're passing on something you don't fully understand, you won't explain it right the first time. Now the tech's own words go straight onto the board.

One voice note fixed a pile of problems.
