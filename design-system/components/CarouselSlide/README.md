# CarouselSlide

A 1080 × 1350 LinkedIn carousel slide in three kinds: hook, content and call to action.

Intentional addition: the website has no carousels; this applies the site's tokens to the 4:5 social format.

- **Hook** (slide 1): `hinterland-sky.webp` full-bleed under the sky overlay, a `headline` statement at about 104px with one `accent-italic` word and one `marker` underline, an `eyebrow` above it.
- **Content**: `background` fill, a big `foreground` headline (64–80px), up to 30 words of `lede`-style body in `muted-foreground`, an optional visual panel in `muted` or `card-secondary` with `radius-4xl`.
- **Call to action** (last slide): the sky again, one sentence, a primary pill, the URL, and André's photo.
- Every slide: footer inside `slide-margin` with the logo at 56px, "Hinterland Web" in `label`, and the slide count as `03 / 08` in `mono-tag`.
- The consumer provides the slide kind, the copy and any visual. Export all slides as one PDF.
