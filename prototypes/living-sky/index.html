How it works

One WebGL canvas between the existing background and the navy gradient. No libraries.

Flow
Each pixel of the sky drifts along a slow noise field. Two offset copies cross-fade on a 12-second loop, so the paint keeps moving without smearing out.
Swirl
The big spiral top-left gets its own gentle back-and-forth turn, so it reads as the painting breathing rather than a filter.
Mask
A soft hand-traced mask (sky-mask.png, 16 KB) keeps the peak and the plains perfectly still. Toggle "Show moving area" to see it.
Stir
Cursor speed adds a small vortex where you point. It eases out a second after you stop.
Loading
The still hero.webp paints first, so load speed is unchanged. The canvas fades in once it has drawn a frame, and stops rendering when the hero is off screen.
Access
Visitors with reduced motion keep the still image. Without WebGL, nothing changes.
